A developer who reads the spec twice.

I started out breaking my own websites just to understand how they were built, and never really stopped. These days that means writing interfaces that are quick to load, easy to use, and animated with a reason behind every motion — not just because it was possible.

I care about the details most visitors won't consciously notice: the timing of a hover state, the weight of a heading, the difference between an interface that works and one that feels considered.
